# Getting started

How to open the project, run it, and play the first hour.

---

## 1. What you need

- Unity **6000.5.8f1**, and it has to be that or newer. The manifest carries
  `com.unity.modules.physicscore2d`, which does not exist before 6000.5, so an older editor
  dies on package resolution and reports `Aborting batchmode` with no test results at all.
  That failure looks exactly like your new test file not being picked up.
- Nothing else. No packages to install by hand, no art to import, no scene to assemble.

## 2. Open it

Unity Hub, **Add** -> **Add project from disk** -> pick the `ScalingLaws` folder (the one with
`Assets`, `Packages` and `ProjectSettings` in it).

First open takes a few minutes because Unity builds its `Library` folder from scratch. That folder
is not in the repository, which is correct: it is a cache, not source.

## 3. Press play

Open `Assets/_ScalingLaws/Scenes/MainMenu.unity` and press play.

If the scenes are missing or look wrong, rebuild them from the menu: **Scaling Laws -> Rebuild
scenes**. Both scenes are generated by code, so this is always safe and always the fix. Anything
you change by hand in the scene view is lost the next time it runs.

## 4. The first hour

**The cold open.** Black screen, January 2022, six lines about what is about to happen to this
industry. Press CONTINUE whenever you have read enough.

**Who you are.** Eight founder traits, pick two. Every one is a trade and none of them is strictly
best. Solopreneur is cheap to run and slow to research. Entrepreneur is the reverse. Researcher
trains better models and cannot sell them. If you want an easy start, take Silver Tongue and
Hardware Whisperer.

**Which lab.** Four companies. OpenSI has the most money and the highest burn. Antropic starts two
Safety levels ahead, which matters far more later than it looks now. DeepSearch is the strongest
research and the weakest brand. HuggyFace is cheap, open and thin on margin. Then a name, then
BEGIN.

**Now you are in the game, paused, on 1 January 2022.** The bottom bar runs the clock. Start on
`>>` and pause whenever something needs thinking about.

### The opening sequence that works

1. **NEW MODEL.** Drag the rented capacity slider to around 150 PF. Watch the projection panel on
   the right fill in. Set parameters to about 20B and press MATCH THE OPTIMUM to get the token
   count right. The verdict box should say "Runnable and well shaped". Press START TRAINING.
2. **RESEARCH.** Start `Corpus curation` immediately. It is cheap, it is fast, and every later node
   depends on it. An idle research org is the most expensive thing a company can have.
3. Unpause and let it run. The first model takes roughly five months.
4. **RELEASE.** When the run finishes it goes on the shelf, not to market. Ship it the same day
   unless you have a reason not to; market par rises under a model that sits unreleased.
5. Revenue starts. **FUNDING** -> OPEN A ROUND -> sign it. In 2022 investor sentiment is 0.55, which
   is the worst it ever gets, so this round is expensive in equity. Take it anyway; you cannot reach
   2024 without it.
6. **Hire.** The garage holds four desks. One research scientist tightens every future run. One
   infrastructure engineer makes the fleet you are already paying for actually deliver.

### What will kill you

- **Renting compute and not using it.** The meter runs whether or not anything is training.
- **Never optimising.** Optimisation and Speed fall behind the market fastest. A model leading on
  capability and two levels behind on Optimisation is losing money on every token it serves.
- **Skipping Mixture of Experts.** It arrives in the tree in late 2023 and cuts serving cost by
  roughly four times. Companies that skip it die in 2025 without understanding why.
- **Ignoring Safety once you are good.** Incident risk scales with capability. A weak model
  misbehaving is a support ticket. A frontier model misbehaving is a fine and sometimes a
  withdrawal.
- **The sovereign loan.** Ten billion in, twenty two and a half billion out over eleven years, and
  the schedule does not pause for a bad quarter.

## 5. Running the tests

From a terminal, with Unity closed:

```
"C:\Program Files\Unity\Hub\Editor\6000.5.8f1\Editor\Unity.exe" -batchmode -nographics -projectPath "<project>" -runTests -testPlatform EditMode -testResults TestResults.xml
```

1077 tests, a few minutes including the import. Exit code 0 means everything passed, 2 means something failed and
`TestResults.xml` says what.

**Do not add `-quit` to `-runTests`.** Unity finishes the import, never runs the tests, and exits 0,
which looks like success and is not.

To rebuild the scenes from a terminal:

```
"C:\Program Files\Unity\Hub\Editor\6000.5.8f1\Editor\Unity.exe" -batchmode -nographics -projectPath "<project>" -executeMethod ScalingLaws.Editor.ScalingLawsSceneBuilder.BuildAll -quit
```

## 6. Where things are

Saves live in PlayerPrefs under `ScalingLaws.Campaign`. Deleting a campaign from the main menu
clears it. The save format is at version 8 and every older version still loads, so a campaign
started on an earlier build is not lost.

`Docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` is the map of every mechanism and the first thing to read before changing
anything. `Docs/UI_RESEARCH_AND_ASSETS.md` is the brief for art.

## 7. Known gaps

- No art. Cards are coloured blocks and logos are letters.
- No hardware or datacenter screen. The simulation is complete, the UI is not, so owned compute can
  only be reached from code for now.
- No staff or office screen yet either, for the same reason.
- The era after superintelligence is a research node with a warning on it, not a set of systems.
