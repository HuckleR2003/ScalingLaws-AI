IN ENGLISH

The game is free and stays free in this form. Steam is a separate matter: putting a game there
means paying Valve's registration fee first. That is the whole reason for this fund.

There is a support button and a counter on the game's page and on GitHub. The counter shows what
has come in so far, and I keep it current, so you are looking at the same figure I am.

Every contribution brings the launch closer. From about ten dollars you are in the supporter pack:
your name or handle in the game's credits and in the SUPPORTERS section of the menu, and a message
from me the day it goes live on Steam.

The fund runs until 19 October 2026. If it does not reach the target, nothing is taken away from
the game: it keeps arriving here, on the site and on itch.io, exactly as it has been.

Support: https://github.com/sponsors/HuckleR2003
The game: https://pcworkman.dev/hck-labs/scaling-laws/

If you would rather not pay, or cannot, and still want to help: play it, tell me what is unclear
or what breaks, and mention it to somebody who likes tycoons. That helps just as much.
