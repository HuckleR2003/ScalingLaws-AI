Models in this folder are from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com), released under
Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal: free for any use, no attribution required.

Each folder holds the 1K FBX as downloaded. Textures were converted: <material>_Color.jpg and
<material>_NormalGL.jpg from the diffuse and OpenGL normal maps, and <material>_MetallicSmoothness.png
(red = metal, alpha = 1 - roughness) for Unity's Standard shader. Downloaded 2026-09-19.
